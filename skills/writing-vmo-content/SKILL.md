# Writing VMO Content

Read `VMO35.md` first.

Every important section must connect:
Business Problem → Execution → Outcome.

Keep Proof → Process → Scale.
Technology follows workflow.
VMO OS is infrastructure, not public SaaS.

No invented proof, numbers, customer names or awards.
Fit copy into Mortar before changing layout.
