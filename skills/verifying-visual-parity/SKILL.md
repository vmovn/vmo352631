# Visual Parity Verification

Compare original vs current at 390px and 1440px.

Check:
- section count/order;
- composition;
- typography hierarchy;
- imagery;
- whitespace;
- animations;
- sliders/video/chart behavior.

Hard fail when a rich section becomes generic or loses important content/effects.
