# VMO Public Architecture — “Long Mạch”

`vmo.vn` is not a brochure. It is the public entry layer of VMO.

```text
DISCOVER
Capabilities / Solutions / Insights
          ↓
UNDERSTAND
0→1 / 1→2 / 2→N / VMO OS
          ↓
PROVE
Projects / Case Studies / Outcomes
          ↓
ENGAGE
Growth Mission / Contact
          ↓
CAPTURE
Payload Lead + Events
          ↓
EXECUTE
FLOW.VMO → CRM.VMO / SCORE.VMO / PM.VMO
          ↓
LEARN
Outcome / reporting / knowledge
```

Homepage should stay business-first.
Technology appears after business problem, workflow and evidence.
